Vocabularies#

A vocabulary is the set of characters a recognition model can predict. docTR ships more than 100 of them, covering the main writing systems, in doctr.datasets.VOCABS:

from doctr.datasets import VOCABS

print(len(VOCABS["german"]))  # 108
print(VOCABS["german"][-8:])  # äöüßÄÖÜẞ

They are used to:

  • train a recognition model on a language: crnn_vgg16_bn(vocab=VOCABS["german"]), or --vocab german with the training script;

  • restrict a trained model to a subset of its characters with a whitelist.

Tip

Vocabularies are plain strings, so you can combine them: VOCABS["german"] + VOCABS["polish"]. Remove the duplicates while keeping the order with "".join(dict.fromkeys(...)).
