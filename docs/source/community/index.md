# Community

docTR is an open-source project developed in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/mindee/doctr). Whether you need
help, want to share what you built, or would like to contribute, you are welcome here.

## Contribute

Fixing a typo, adding a vocabulary, implementing a paper: every contribution counts. The
{doc}`contribution guide <../contributing/contributing>` explains how to set up a development environment, run the
tests and build this documentation. Please also read the {doc}`code of conduct <../contributing/code_of_conduct>`.

## Professional support

docTR was originally created by [Mindee](https://mindee.com) and is now developed and maintained by
[t2k GmbH](https://www.text2knowledge.de). t2k offers professional services around docTR:

- **Custom OCR development**: detection, recognition and pipeline work tailored to your documents
- **Project implementation**: turning your ideas into production-ready docTR integrations
- **Technical consulting**: architecture, deployment and integration guidance
- **Ongoing development**: feature extensions and implementation support
- **Workshops & advisory**: hands-on sessions and recommendations for working with docTR

Contact [info@text2knowledge.de](mailto:info@text2knowledge.de) or visit [text2knowledge.de](https://www.text2knowledge.de)
to discuss your project.
